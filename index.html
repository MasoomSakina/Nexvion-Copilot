How to Install Nexvion Copilot

Get up and running with your zero-shot AI screen guide in under 30 seconds.

01
ZIP

Download & Extract

Click download above to get your Nexvion-Copilot.zip package, then unzip it to a folder.

02
URL

Open Extensions

Go to Chrome and paste chrome://extensions/ in your search bar. Enable Developer mode.

03
AI

Load & Launch

Click "Load unpacked" in the top left corner, select your extracted folder, and your copilot is ready!
